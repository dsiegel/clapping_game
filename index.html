👏 Clapping Game

Levels

Notes cross the green line exactly on the beat — clap (or press SPACE, or tap the screen) so your tick lands on the note. Keep scoring green and the game levels up: first the small in-between notes disappear, then the beats themselves, then the mode starts switching randomly at the top of each measure (watch the banner). Slip up and you drop a level. If claps aren't registering, drag sensitivity right. First 4 beats are a count-in — stay quiet during it: the app listens to its own clicks to measure your audio latency. Green = within ±50 ms, gold = within ±20 ms.
